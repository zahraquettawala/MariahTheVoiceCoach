# MariahTheVoiceCoach
AI voice coach for singers: daily agility warmups, real-time pitch scoring, and an ElevenLabs voice agent as your teacher.

## Try it

Open **https://zahraquettawala.github.io/MariahTheVoiceCoach/** on your phone in Safari, tap **Start**, allow the microphone, and sing.

## Status

Step 1 of the build: a pitch test. The page shows the note you're singing, a too low / on it / too high meter, and a line of your pitch over the last few seconds. Pitch detection runs in the browser with [Pitchy](https://github.com/ianprime0509/pitchy). Nothing is recorded or saved; the sound stays on your phone.
